Institutional Masterplan

3-Year Strategic Implementation Roadmap

Structured development timeline (2025 – 2028) focused on digital literacy, scholarship funding, and agricultural modernization.

Y1
Phase 1: Foundation (2025-2026)

Digital Infrastructure & AI Access

  • Establish MBANSA AI Learning Hub for regional schools.
  • Launch central database for verified student registration.
  • Distribute initial educational grants to 100 scholars.
Y2
Phase 2: Expansion (2026-2027)

Agritech & STEM Labs

  • Construct 2 community computer labs in Mbam and Nkum.
  • Roll out practical agricultural technology workshops for youth.
  • Form diaspora mentorship matching network.
Y3
Phase 3: Sustainability (2027-2028)

Enterprise & Endowment

  • Establish MBANSA Revolving Student Micro-Grant Fund.
  • Partner with national technical universities for placements.
  • Achieve 100% self-sustaining institutional financing model.
Focus Areas

Community Impact Sectors

Tailored development strategies built specifically for the local socio-economic dynamics of Bui Division.

Academic Excellence

Scholarship awards, national exam preparation, and textbook library access for secondary and tertiary students.

Agritech & Sustainable Farming

Integrating modern soil science and AI-driven agricultural tools to empower young farmers in Mbam and Nkum.

Digital Literacy & Code

Hands-on software development bootcamps and computer literacy classes held across local community halls.

Cultural & Social Leadership

Fostering unity, civic engagement, and historical preservation among students from Mbam and Nkum origins.
